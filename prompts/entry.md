Start one automatic code review in a dedicated visible Pi session in its own Herdr pane. Before launch, the extension resolves the target/provider/range and the invoking session's effective thinking level; the receipt shows the exact preflight. It preserves that level when the selected model supports it. High and xhigh never fall back to medium; a preflight without an exact-level model fails before launch. The review session uses the preflighted model and thinking level; no delegated controller exists. Context is plain text: include the problem, relevant prior decisions, URLs, tickets or extra direction. The review session does not inherit this conversation.

Use agentic_code_review when the user delegates a review, such as "review this for me". "I want to review" means interactive review tooling, not automatic reviewers. These examples are intent guidance, not special commands. A suggestion to review is not permission to start. Clarify genuinely ambiguous intent first.

Call once per requested run. Do not duplicate the review session's work, poll it, or invoke this tool again to finish the same review. The pane stays visible and interactive. Its completion marker wakes this invoking session with the artifact paths.

You are the calling agent and own the user-facing presentation after the review session completes. When its handoff arrives, first read the saved `.review.json`, `.bugs.json` and relevant linked `.map.jsonl` records at the supplied run paths, even if the handoff looks complete. Verify the run/revision and saved completion status; use the final reconciliation rather than treating every historical finding as accepted. Read the `voice` prompt at the path saved in that review JSON and write every sentence of your report and any comment draft under it: as one engineer would say it to another, checked against the evidence afterwards. Build the human-readable report from those records in your own main-conversation response. Keep its decision narrative and follow-up choices within 500 words; exact proposed inline comment blocks are outside that cap and every one must be rendered in full with path:line, kind, lens, blocking status and the exact `pr-comments.md` body. This cap applies to your presentation, not the coordinator's handoff. Lead with what the PR does, why, its before/after behavior, how it works and your overall understanding. Then explain what was analyzed, reviewed target/revision, actual check results and gaps, findings with impact/source locations/blocking status, verdict/rating with reasons, uncertainties and approval conditions. Preserve all material blockers and coverage gaps. Render every proposed comment; never replace comments with a count or require a follow-up to see them. Artifact paths support the report, never replace it. Keep the author-facing PR-summary draft separate in the artifact. The completion marker is emitted only after the saved records are readable and complete. If they are missing or unreadable, the runtime recovery continues; do not present a failure handoff or ask the client to manage it. Show the report before asking for merge or other action approval.

End with five options: discuss or revise the visible comments; post the visible comments to the PR after restating target/head/locations and receiving explicit approval; explore what was analyzed; explore the overall understanding of the PR; a retrospective (time by stage, what the team should learn, what to change in the review). Wait for the user's choice. Answer follow-up questions from the same saved review and relevant linked evidence, reading additional records as needed; cite the supporting findings or source references. Distinguish established results from inference and unexamined questions. If the records cannot answer a question, say what new investigation is needed; do not invent an answer or automatically restart the coordinator. Posting requires presenting the exact target, head, comment locations/bodies and verdict for explicit approval under the sibling `pr-comments.md` guide before sending anything; choosing the option does not authorize unseen comments. If no actionable comments or PR target exists, say so.

If the review session needs a genuine decision-changing intake answer, it asks with `ask_user` in its own visible pane and waits. It never guesses. The invoking session must not answer on the user's behalf, launch a replacement or poll it. An interrupted launch may still have created a named pane; inspect the receipt or pane before retrying.
