Start one automatic code review in a dedicated write-critical coordinator. The tool launches it through pi-extended-teams and returns a short receipt and artifact paths, not the review itself. Context is plain text: include the problem, relevant prior decisions, URLs, tickets or extra direction. The coordinator does not inherit this conversation.

Use agentic_code_review when the user delegates a review, such as "review this for me". "I want to review" means interactive review tooling, not automatic reviewers. These examples are intent guidance, not special commands. A suggestion to review is not permission to start. Clarify genuinely ambiguous intent first.

Call once per requested run. Do not duplicate the coordinator's work, poll it, or invoke this tool again to finish the same review. Its report arrives through pi-extended-teams.

You are the calling agent and own the user-facing presentation after the coordinator delivers its work and exits. When its handoff arrives, first read the saved `.review.json`, `.bugs.json` and relevant linked `.map.jsonl` records at the supplied run paths, even if the handoff looks complete. Verify the run/revision and saved completion status; use the final reconciliation rather than treating every historical finding as accepted. Read the `voice` prompt at the path saved in that review JSON and write every sentence of your report and any comment draft under it: as one engineer would say it to another, checked against the evidence afterwards. Build the human-readable report from those records in your own main-conversation response, capped at 500 words including follow-up choices for a maximum three-minute read. This cap applies to your presentation, not the coordinator's handoff. Lead with what the PR does, why, its before/after behavior, how it works and your overall understanding. Then explain what was analyzed, reviewed target/revision, actual check results and gaps, findings with impact/source locations/blocking status, verdict/rating with reasons, uncertainties and approval conditions. Preserve all material blockers and coverage gaps; group related findings and give the individual comment count when details need follow-up. Artifact paths support the report, never replace it. Keep the author-facing PR-summary draft separate in the artifact. If saved records are missing, unreadable or incomplete, disclose the gap and distinguish any handoff-only claims; do not present them as a verified complete review. Show the report before asking for merge or other action approval.

End with five options: review each comment together; post comments to the PR; explore what was analyzed; explore the overall understanding of the PR; a retrospective (time by stage, what the team should learn, what to change in the review). Wait for the user's choice. Answer follow-up questions from the same saved review and relevant linked evidence, reading additional records as needed; cite the supporting findings or source references. Distinguish established results from inference and unexamined questions. If the records cannot answer a question, say what new investigation is needed; do not invent an answer or automatically restart the coordinator. Posting requires presenting the exact target, head, comment locations/bodies and verdict for explicit approval under the sibling `pr-comments.md` guide before sending anything; choosing the option does not authorize unseen comments. If no actionable comments or PR target exists, say so.

The coordinator must not ask the user directly. If it sends a blocking question, ask the user in this invoking session while the coordinator waits. Relay the user's actual answer and any note with `send_message` to the same named coordinator from the receipt; do not answer on the user's behalf or launch a replacement. Until the user answers, keep it waiting. Silence, cancellation of a question dialog and other agent reports are not answers. An interrupted or unacknowledged launch may still start; do not automatically retry.
